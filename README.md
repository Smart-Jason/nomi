# Nomi

**A little company for your desktop.**

[Download for Windows](https://github.com/Smart-Jason/nomi/releases/latest) | [Website](https://smart-jason.github.io/nomi/) | [中文说明](releases/v1.1.0.zh-CN.md)

![Nomi desktop companion promotional artwork](assets/nomi-world.png)

Nomi is a free AI companion for Windows that brings conversation and a little warmth to your desktop. Chat, share photos, exchange letters, and make Nomi feel like your own companion.

## Meet Nomi

- **Your own desktop companion.** Choose an image or Live2D appearance, and personalize Nomi's name, personality, and theme.
- **Conversations, your way.** Chat with local or supported online AI models, and share images with a compatible model.
- **Voice and camera calls.** Speak with Nomi, or add camera snapshots to a turn-based call using a local vision model.
- **Letters for everyday moments.** Receive a welcome letter and daily notes, or write a letter to your future self.
- **A place for shared moments.** Browse photos, add notes, create memory cards, revisit your companion journal, and find past chats, photos, and letters with local search.

## Download and Setup

1. Download the `.exe` installer from the [latest release](https://github.com/Smart-Jason/nomi/releases/latest).
2. Install and open Nomi. Choose a resource folder and download the basic models and tools.
3. Select **Start Nuomi** and start chatting.

The Windows installer is about **200 MiB**. Models and tools download separately on first launch, so allow additional disk space and an internet connection. Spoken replies use online Edge TTS by default.

The interface starts in English. You can choose Chinese in the launcher or app settings.
