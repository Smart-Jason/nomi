# Nomi v1.1.0

A little company for your desktop. Nomi is a free AI companion for Windows.

## Highlights

- Text chat with local or online AI, and image conversations with a compatible model.
- Voice conversations, plus camera-assisted, turn-based calls using a local vision model.
- A personal Inbox for welcome letters, daily notes, and letters to your future self.
- A photo library, local search, and a companion journal.
- Customizable names, personality, themes, and image or Live2D appearances.

## Getting started

1. Download [Nuomi Setup 1.1.0.exe](https://github.com/Smart-Jason/nomi/releases/download/v1.1.0/Nuomi.Setup.1.1.0.exe) and run the installer.
2. Open Nomi, choose where to save resources, and download the basic models and tools.
3. Select **Start Nuomi** and start chatting. You can switch between English and Chinese in settings.

**Windows. Installer: about 200 MiB.** Models and tools download separately on first launch, so allow extra disk space and an internet connection.
